    Describe an edit
    
          
          
          
    
            
            
            
          
    
          
    
            Enter to generate. Shift+Enter for a new line.
          
    
          
    
            Talk records up to 30 seconds. Finish sends the audio to Gemini and
            applies the spoken edit. Cancel stops recording or transcription. At
            the time limit, the transcript is kept for review; press Generate to
            apply it. An existing draft requires Replace draft before recording;
            Keep draft cancels without changing it. Nothing records
            automatically.
