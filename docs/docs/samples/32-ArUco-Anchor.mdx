## Printed marker setup

This demo detects ArUco markers and starts on the `ARUCO_MIP_36h12` dictionary,
ID **0**, with a physical width of **150 mm (0.15 m)**: that is the side of the
printed **black square**, not the paper size. Print one from the js-aruco2
[marker creator](https://damianofalcioni.github.io/js-aruco2/samples/marker-creator/marker-creator.html?dictionary=ARUCO_MIP_36h12),
keep its white margin, and measure the print.

Use the panel's dictionary button to switch between `ARUCO_MIP_36h12` (250
IDs, error-correcting) and the original `ARUCO` dictionary (1023 IDs), and its
− and + controls to select another ID. The three axes are centered on the
marker: X is red, Y is green, and Z is blue.

On desktop, add `?simMarker` to the URL to hang a virtual print in the
simulator's room.

## Persistent anchor behavior

After the selected marker is observed, the demo keeps its last measured world
pose when the marker is outside the camera view. A later valid observation
smoothly refreshes that cached pose. Select a different marker or use **Reset
anchor** to discard the current anchor deliberately.
